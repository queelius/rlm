## Research threads

1. **Interface competence and task-conditioned supervision (Aug 24 to 29).** SFT taught a Qwen controller to use the Python ABI; a hidden-task curriculum failed and a generic dispatcher repaired it; narrow success did not transfer to delegation workflows.
2. **Helper classification, record correspondence and call size (Sept 8 to 12).** TREC/news/NLI helpers; matching record IDs fix large-batch misalignment; small unkeyed calls are strong baselines; group contents and request size change labels.
3. **Root/controller SFT and compound-operation execution (Sept 9 to 11).** Demonstrations taught faithful two-stage calculations on described compound operations; plan was supplied, not discovered.
4. **Reward-based learning for root and helper (Sept 9 to 12).** Many one-to-eight-update pilots; real weight changes, small same-panel gains, repeated attenuation on fresh panels and against extra SFT.
5. **Helper-information diagnostics (Sept 12).** Oracle labels / replay controls show the root often calculates correctly from wrong helper labels; better local labels do not automatically fix the whole task.
6. **Serving and training numerical infrastructure (Sept 12).** vLLM batch-invariant mode, importance-weight qualification gates, replay checks.
7. **MRCR conversation retrieval (Sept 12 to 13).** Procedural SFT learned a Python search routine that transfers to longer inputs and later occurrences; a whitespace-trimming return path hid correct answers; final-token RL mostly improved delivery and one gain did not replicate.
8. **Fixed-call-graph multi-hop QA screens (Sept 12).** MuSiQue follow-up, allocation, summary-versus-original and relation-extraction screens: no exact-answer gains.
9. **Generated record-selection task, b05 (Sept 12 to 13).** Python-organized state and one-helper-per-candidate (singleton delegation) improved complete answers on fresh cases; selection RL gave small mixed changes.
10. **Unattended breadth campaign and FinQA arithmetic (Sept 14 to 15; audited Sept 29).** Fixed splitting/summarizing did not consistently help across five datasets; code-executed arithmetic roughly doubled strict FinQA matches.
11. **Learned QA decomposition / selective delegation (Sept 21 to 29).** Trained MuSiQue planner and helper, sufficiency (answer/refuse) training, QAMPARI fan-out, ALFWorld manager: no cost-justified architecture advantage on fresh panels.
12. **TextCraft teaching and teaching-history repair (Sept 22 to Oct 19).** Discovery teaching, answer-preserving history repair, extra-training dose, Phi second family; repair reliably beats the weak original teacher, not the stronger controls.
13. **TextCraft execution assistance and state displays (Sept 24 to 28).** Code-filled ingredient arguments help; recipe notebook and demand table do not.
14. **TextCraft RL and reward-contrast diagnosis (Sept 28 to Oct 14).** Four fixed updates give no sustained gain; four training goals never succeed; compact-action RL is a small positive to replicate.
15. **TextCraft delegation (Sept 28 to 29).** Children execute but root benefit is unmeasured.
16. **Oct 14 to 19 teaching campaign.** Fresh-structure pilot, recovery-versus-ordinary teaching, second training seed, two broader worlds.
