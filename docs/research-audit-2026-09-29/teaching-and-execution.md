The defensible result is a controlled, finite-budget demonstration-repair finding, accompanied by a separate execution-interface gain. A correct oracle demonstration can be difficult for the tested learner to imitate usefully. Moving recipe queries until their item names have appeared publicly substantially improves task completion while preserving every taught answer and every optimizer minibatch's answer-token denominator. This survives a second fit seed and additional exposed roots. It does not yet isolate observability from copying, history content, recency or input length; establish an asymptotic advantage; or demonstrate recursive learning.

The other established result is that filling ingredient arguments from previously queried recipes helps an already useful discovery-trained actor. This removes many command-construction errors but leaves discovery, stock accounting and goal completion unresolved. A completed computed-demand display pilot scored **0/8 versus 0/8**; its README's preparation-only status is stale. A public quantity ledger is therefore a tested unsuccessful package on that small slice, not a pending first experiment or an established remedy.

## What the environment and teacher actually know

This is a custom strict-JSON action wrapper around selected native TextCraft-Synth transition and scoring methods from [Platoon](https://github.com/ApGa/platoon), pinned to commit `d9c5857d3a0a056ebc9b047241a2a0c9515aafbe` (MIT). It recompiles reviewed, allowlisted `craft`, `get_info`, `view_inventory` and evaluation method bodies; it does not run the complete Platoon/IPython lifecycle. The actor observes the goal, initial/current inventory and its action/feedback history. An item lookup returns that item's recipe. `can_craft` means **recipe exists**, not **current stock suffices**. Goals require the requested net inventory increase and explicit finish. See [CPU bridge qualification](../../experiments/selective_delegation/TEXTCRAFT-CPU-READINESS.md).

The original known-recipe teacher has an offline gold plan and often names an intermediate before any public reply has revealed it. Arbitrary name queries are legal: an unseen name is neither an illegal action nor automatically unpredictable in a fixed, memorisable world. The discovery teacher obtains dependencies through public root-first lookup. The later repair scheduler is different again: it reorders an **oracle-supplied action list**, retaining original crafts and quantities, while requiring names to be publicly visible before querying them. It does not independently discover which gold crafts or quantities to include. Native feasibility replay is an audit, not an extra oracle input to the student.

The source uses official synthetic task IDs and generator semantics, but the small selected/stratified panels, wrapper, caps and altered recipe worlds are not an official benchmark aggregate or reproduction of the [RAO evaluation](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.06639v1). World42→43 preserves all 1,452 item names while changing 1,399/1,402 recipes and 326 batch sizes; feasible initial stock is reconstructed. This tests recipe/inventory changes under the same vocabulary and grammar, not name-independent or difficulty-matched transfer. Counterfactual hybrid worlds are explicitly constructed tasks. Root disjointness does not remove shared intermediate recipes. These boundaries are recorded in the [transfer note](../../experiments/selective_delegation/TEXTCRAFT-PUBLIC-DISCOVERY-TRANSFER.md).

## The strongest repair evidence and remaining alternatives

All repair fits use the same 32 TRAIN tasks, 366 target strings, 8,820 JSON+EOS answer tokens, checkpoint23, matching initialization and original known-teacher row order. Each of the 23 actual minibatch answer-token denominators is identical. Every original craft remains in its original native order. Publicly visible query names increase from 32/167 to 167/167. Stable and seeded-random eligible-query schedules both work, and each reproduces the discovery teacher's full native sequence on only 8/32 TRAIN tasks. These controls weaken explanations based on different answers, optimizer row order, one lucky fit, or one special discovery action sequence.

Stable-minus-discovery is +3.12 points (−4.69 to +10.94): no superiority or equivalence is established. The added measurements were fixed after the pilot and use previously exposed breadth panels. Sixteen distinct roots, common recipes and adaptive design limit inferential scope. Input supervision is not matched: known/stable/random have 414,682/431,274/435,710 prompt tokens. The intervention also changes previous observations, action recency and native positions. Copying an available identifier may be easier than retrieving it from model weights; the current study cannot distinguish that from a general learned information-gathering strategy. Stable and random rescue do not prove a visibility-only cause.

The dose control is consequential: known reaches 8/32 at both update46 and update69; discovery reaches 14→15→17/32. Fixed world-level known results swap 3+5 to 5+3, so a best-checkpoint-per-world summary would manufacture 10 successes. Stable at update23 remains 14/32, but stable23-minus-known69 is only +18.75 points with interval [0,+40.62]; random23-minus-known69 is +12.50 [−9.38,+37.50]. Training-state durations are approximately 182→364→545 seconds for known and 184 seconds for stable23, not owner wall time or matched FLOPs. The promising claim is efficiency at these doses; teacher-forced endpoint NLL remains an uncompleted diagnostic in the inspected dose package.

The second model family does not yet replicate repair. Phi-4-mini-instruct gives known/discovery raw **1/16 versus 4/16**, and binder **0/16 versus 6/16**, across eight roots and two worlds with one rollout seed. Discovery's binder gains are one root, VAL263, in both worlds. Equal updates across model families do not equalize tokenizer labels, LoRA parameter dimensions or compute. The [Phi repair package](../../experiments/selective_delegation/phi_repair_20260929/README.md) is prepared, with pending readout plans but no completed repaired actor/report at inspection. Do not describe seed2291 of Qwen as a second model family, or Phi's original-package comparison as repair transfer.

The [CPU counterfactual witness](../../experiments/selective_delegation/teacher_counterfactual_20260928/README.md) sharply limits the causal story: all eight matched public prefixes have conflicting oracle query labels, but **48/48** bounded oracle/public continuations succeed. Thirteen oracle-forced continuations cost one extra query; none cost more. All16 oracle next-query names are absent from the shared prompt, so this is not a conflict among already-visible candidates. This shows literal teacher-label ambiguity can be harmless for success. It uses artificial world completions, ample common stock and a capable deterministic continuation, so it neither proves model recoverability nor explains the SFT gap. Diagnostic B stays diagnostic; these observations must not choose training examples, training objectives or checkpoints.

A legitimate longitudinal connection to the parent's August24 v2a/v2b audit is the question **whether decision supervision is conditioned on the available facts**. The earlier same-bootstrap-input/incompatible-operation targets and later hidden-name query schedules are not an established common causal mechanism. In TextCraft, alternative initial query labels can still yield success, and the original single-world training inputs need not contain contradictions.

## Execution assistance helps, then reaches a different boundary

The binder substitutes the exact ingredient dictionary from one unambiguous, previously observed recipe only when the model's chosen output amount is batch-divisible. Target and quantity stay model-chosen. It adds no recipe queries, hidden facts, subgoals or automatic finish. Executed actions and native feedback enter later histories; raw requests remain separately recorded. It is automatic argument construction, not learned decomposition.

Early world47/world48 results were 6→12, 8→10, 9→14 and, in the completed fourth cell, **9→13 successes of16**. The fourth cell's source is `R/analysis-textcraft-world48-seed2026092291-public-recipe-binder-followup-001.json` (4 wins, 0 losses). The [September24 summary](../../experiments/selective_delegation/RECIPE-BINDING-RESULTS-20260924.md) predates that cell and should not be treated as current completion status.

Breadth provides stronger and less flattering evidence: discovery raw→binder **323→381/768**, 74 wins and 16 losses, +7.55 points (root-cluster interval +5.08 to +10.16). Known raw→binder is only **39→48/768**, +1.17 points (−0.26 to +2.86). Thus the interface helps a policy that discovers useful facts more than one that often fails to query its goal. This is consistent with complementary teaching and execution improvements, not proof of a unique internal mechanism.

Discovery ingredient-construction errors fall 5,953→281, while insufficient-stock errors rise 3,155→5,132. Error precedence and changed trajectories prevent reading this as worse planning caused by the binder. Depth5 completion remains only **37/288**, versus depth2 **181/192**. Small early panels' absence of paired losses did not persist under breadth.

The [world47 traces](../../experiments/selective_delegation/WORLD47-CONTRASTS-20260924.md) make the distinction concrete. A corrected-known actor makes the necessary parts for VAL629 and then finishes without assembly; a public actor assembles the root. Conversely, on VAL47 the public actor repeatedly adds an extra ingredient despite having queried the correct recipe; the corrected-known actor recovers and succeeds. Corrected-known makes far fewer action errors (43 versus312) but completes fewer tasks (4 versus6 of16). Neither a valid command nor a root-first query is a reliable proxy for finishing the goal.

The retrospective [inventory audit](../../experiments/selective_delegation/inventory_bottleneck_20260928/FINDING.md) covers all 24 depth4/5 identities in breadth panels00–05: discovery binder **76/384**, raw **38/384**. Among 308 binder failures, 152 have complete observed recipe closure, 147 have a completion constructively feasible from public recipes/stock, 277 leave some needed prerequisite immediately craftable, and 222 include repeated insufficient-stock errors. Missing recipes remain substantial; public feasibility is not evidence that the actor understood the plan. Only four successful crafts demonstrably change public completion feasibility from true to false, so irreversible exhaustion is not supported as the dominant witnessed failure. Inventory equality is not full policy-state equality: history and remaining budget also matter.

Two intervention results constrain the obvious remedies. The fact-only recipe notebook did not improve full-history completion: original fit **15→14/32**, second fit **15→6/32**, historical RL actor **14→14/32**. The separate computed-demand experiment also completed: **demand 0/8 versus token-length-matched masked 0/8**, with six versus five context caps and 106 versus90 duplicate stock-error calls. Every episode has a ready prerequisite; five failures per arm remain publicly completable. Demand costs 418 calls/15,686 output tokens versus413/13,055. Both tables and padding consume the unchanged context budget. This rules out claiming success for this presentation package; it does not establish equivalence or show that arithmetic assistance can never help. The bootstrap's all-zero [0,0] interval is degenerate and must not be interpreted as a precise population null.

## Novelty boundary and next discriminating comparisons

The local primary-method reviews already identify close antecedents: [Student-Informed Teacher Training](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.09149v2) and [Robust Asymmetric Learning in POMDPs](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/warrington21a/warrington21a.pdf) adapt privileged teaching to partially informed students; [Guided Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.15418v2) includes an information-gathering counterexample; [Privileged Information Distillation for Language Models](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.04942v3) addresses multi-turn/action-only LM distillation. [Causal Confusion in Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/html/1905.11979v2) already compares identical demonstrations under altered observation conditioning, and [Adaptive Information Gathering via Imitation Learning](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss13/p41.pdf) learns queries from clairvoyant oracles. General information mismatch, query-first imitation, altered conditioning and demonstration repair are not new claims. The potential contribution is narrower: a native-executable offline history transformation with fixed literal answers and actual optimizer minibatch targets, plus replicated task effects and explicit failure boundaries. This audit uses the recorded [method-level review](../../experiments/selective_delegation/TEACHER-OBSERVABILITY-METHODS-REVIEW-20260928.md) and [additional primary-source review](../../experiments/selective_delegation/teaching_prior_art_20260929/README.md); no new literature search or global-priority claim was needed.

1. **Complete the already prepared Phi stable repair comparison.** Same known-teacher target strings and row order, native Phi token labels, fixed seed/update23; raw worlds42/50, eight roots and one rollout seed/world, paired to existing known/discovery. Native Phi answer tokens are exactly matched at9,081; prompt tokens rise405,612→421,776. Estimated one-A100 cost15–30 minutes, with existing30-minute owner caps. A positive result supports repair-package transfer; a null narrows the claim to Qwen/current fit. No binder expansion is needed to answer this first question.
2. **Test item-identity dependence with the prepared name-permutation design.** Existing known and stable checkpoint23 actors, panel00/world42, one fixed rollout seed:16 new attempts under one frozen episode-consistent bijection; reuse16 identity controls only if the complete inference contract matches. Encode/decode goals, inventory, replies and actions consistently, preserving native transitions and scoring. CPU-check equivalence before collection; keep96 calls,8,192 context/generated-token budgets and256 tokens/call. Estimate30–60 minutes on one A100, cap90. A retained advantage weakens exact-name dependence; selective loss narrows transfer; collapse of both leaves rendering/tokenization shift as an alternative. It does not uniquely distinguish copying from evidence use or warrant an unseen-composition claim.
3. **Condition recovery teaching on the parent's fresh-A evidence, then test it against ordinary teaching.** The [A-only preparation](../../experiments/selective_delegation/recovery_teaching_prepare_20260929/README.md) already supplies32 rows/8 A roots per arm,7 queries/17 crafts/8 finishes and823 target tokens each. Recovery starts immediately after each A repeat0 first native error; selection does not depend on final success. Ordinary rows match task/action type and target length. Only24/32 labels are identical; histories and prompt dose differ80,120 versus65,044. The public teacher can complete all16 CPU qualification trajectories, which is feasibility rather than learned efficacy. No trainer or launch-ready adapter exists. After the parent decides from A-only evidence, freeze the intended one-update fit and paired full-start A readouts; any expansion needs a newly frozen unread evaluation slice. Diagnostic B must not select the training, labels or endpoint. Promote only an actual completion gain over ordinary teaching, not reduced errors or teacher feasibility. Budget from the accepted fresh-A runtime/caps before admission; do not invent a cost estimate or treat the earlier RL outcomes as part of this audit.

Do not repeat root-first prompting, a fact notebook, or the public-demand pilot as untried ideas. Do not run more dose grids to avoid confronting transfer. A precise presently unjustified claim is: **“Making expert decisions observable causally teaches a general, model-independent planning algorithm that outperforms adequate original-teacher training on unseen compositional tasks.”** Current evidence supports a useful controlled case study and a promising training-data transformation, with mechanism and portability still open.
