\begin{frame}[plain]
\titlepage
\vfill
\begin{block}{In one sentence}
Moving the decomposition routine out of the weights into a prebound library, and the
direct-versus-decompose choice into a table of verified outcomes (retrieval keyed by the input, as in RAG), lets an \emph{untrained} 4B
controller match or beat every controller we trained, at lower cost, on both task families.
\end{block}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]
\frametitle{Where we were: learned selective recursion, and routing collapse.}
\small
\textbf{Setup.} Qwen3-4B controls a recursive language model (RLM): it writes Python cells, calls
helper models on chunks, and submits an answer. Two families: a synthetic store log (8 to 512
lines, five question forms) and OOLONG-synth (1k to 32k tokens).
\medskip
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.47\textwidth}
\textbf{What worked.} Teaching by replaying a scripted expert, then RL with a verifiable reward:
held-out OOLONG 0.567 (taught) to 0.652 (RL); direct answering 0.208.
\medskip

\textbf{What did not.} Every RL form lost the \emph{route choice} (answer directly on short
inputs, decompose on long ones): absolute and relative cost, KL to the warm start, sequence
normalisation, short-task weighting, stratified sampling, a balance term.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.49\textwidth}
\begin{block}{The decisive pair}
A consistent teacher makes the rule learnable by supervised fine-tuning (direct on 38 of 40 short
tasks, decompose on 31 of 32 long ones). RL started \emph{from that correct router} is all-direct
by step 5 (dev 0.87 to 0.30).
\end{block}
The rule is representable; policy-gradient dynamics move it globally. Routing collapse is also
reported in the literature (Kassenaar et al. 2026).
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\source{Ledger E06, E07-1 to E07-9 in docs/RESULTS.md. One seed per arm except where stated; synthetic families; exact-match scoring.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]
\frametitle{Put the route choice in a table: no training, best accuracy, lowest cost.}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.5\textwidth}
\textbf{The table.} Every logged run on the teaching split contributes (input signature, routine,
correct, tokens). The signature is computed from the input text alone: family, line-count
bucket, question form.
\medskip

\textbf{The note.} At run time the harness retrieves the history for the input's signature and
renders it in the prompt with one recommendation: highest accuracy minus 0.05 per 10k tokens.
\medskip

\textbf{Result.} The untrained controller follows the recommendation on 120 of 120 dev tasks.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.46\textwidth}

\begin{block}{Online, and with a learned key}
Online streams: greedy warm-started from the table \textbf{0.87}, lowest regret. Learned key (the controller's own hidden state + length, nearest neighbours) matches the hand signature on 413 held-out tasks.

\begin{frame}[t]
\frametitle{A library must not overclaim: what happens off its coverage.}
Three question forms the library does not cover were added to the generator (most-buying person,
purchase span, busiest day with a tie rule).
\medskip
\begin{center}\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{.5\textwidth}r>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{.32\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
72 held-out dev tasks & Correct & What the controller does \\
\midrule
Direct answer, no library & 29/72 & fine to 64 lines, near zero above \\
Untrained + shipped library (docstring claims every form) & \alert{0/72} & submits the library's ``unknown'' \\
Untrained + honest library (None off coverage; look first) & 34/72 & writes its own aggregation \\
\quad of which top buyer & 1/24 & right name, wrong format in 20 of 24 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\textbf{Reading.} A trusting controller plus an overclaiming library is worse than no library. An honest
contract recovers most of it; the residual losses, exact output format and budget-safe chunking, are
what a verified routine provides.
\source{Ledger E08-0, E08-1. A first honest variant let the controller guess before it had seen the question (0/3); the fix: look first, aggregate next cell.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]
\frametitle{Grow the library by verification: the librarian loop.}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.5\textwidth}
\small
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item Cache the model's own event extractions for the teaching tasks of the new form.
\item Ask the proposer for candidate routines: event schema, the shipped aggregator as style,
three teaching examples with gold answers.
\item Verify each candidate on the cached extractions against gold; keep the best above threshold.
\item Register it with its verification record; it enters the statistics table like any route.
\end{enumerate}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{.46\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Result: four held-out forms}
Untrained caller on dev, before $\to$ after growth:\\
top buyer 0 $\to$ \textbf{24}/24; purchase span 0 $\to$ \textbf{24}/24 (4B proposer);\\
busiest day 0 $\to$ \textbf{24}/24: every 4B candidate had one sort bug (\alert{0/48}, kept out); an 8B proposer fixed it;\\
join (bought on a day someone else returned) 0 $\to$ \textbf{22}/24 (4B, 43/48 verified; direct answer 6/24).
\end{block}
\small The ceiling is the proposer, not the caller; verification makes mixing capacities safe.
\end{column}
\end{columns}
\source{Runs e08-*, e11-*. Prior art: Voyager, LILO, Agent Workflow Memory.}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]
\frametitle{What this changes, and what to test next.}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\begin{column}{.48\textwidth}
\small
\textbf{The claim, sharpened.} For a small controller, capability belongs in a library of verified
programs over the kernel's ABI, selection in outcome statistics keyed by the input; the weights
only call and compose. RL's remaining job is where verification is unavailable: helper quality,
and proposing routines the librarian then verifies.
\medskip

\textbf{Limits.} Synthetic families; one seed per arm; exact-match scoring; a greedy table keyed
by hand-chosen signature features; growth tested on easy forms first.
\end{column}
