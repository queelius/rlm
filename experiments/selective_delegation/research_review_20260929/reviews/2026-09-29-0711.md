The unchanged objective weights each sampled action's log probability by its
terminal-reward advantage relative to the other attempts on the same goal.
It uses the exact originally emitted tokens, temperature 0.5, normalization by
32 trajectories, and a fresh AdamW step at learning rate 0.00002. The base is
FP16 and trainable adapter parameters are FP32.

On average, training made sampled actions from successful attempts more likely
and those from unsuccessful attempts less likely. This rules out a no-op update
for this run. It does **not** establish improved decisions, task completion,
transfer, or a useful amount of policy change. These are training-batch token
statistics, not KL divergence or an independent evaluation. The negative loss
is an expected property of this signed objective, not a task score.

Nor does the mean token change establish that the update is too small. Whole
actions contain multiple tokens, only some decisions matter for success, and
shared parameters affect actions not directly credited. The fixed task
readouts and subsequent numbered updates are more informative than choosing a
new learning rate from these diagnostics alone.

The previously verified collection remains 5/32 successes with only three of
eight goals providing mixed rewards. Thus a working optimizer does not resolve
the lack of direct contrast on the other five goals. It also does not separate
state coverage, credit assignment, or inventory-planning explanations.
